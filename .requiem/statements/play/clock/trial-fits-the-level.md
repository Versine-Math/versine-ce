A Time Trial asks for as many problems as fit in about five minutes at the level's expected pace (its par time), from 1 to 12, rather than 12 on every level. Complex problems such as working the quadratic formula or several stages of logic take minutes each, so 12 of them was a half-hour trial or an unpassable one. The last level asks for 6 where 6 fit in seven minutes, because it mixes every earlier level and counts as a review. Auto-adjustment still moves the clock; the number of problems is per level, not per student.
