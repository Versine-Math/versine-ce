A student's Time Trial clock stays between 2 and 10 minutes for the whole trial, whatever the number of problems, and typically sits near 5. This replaces the old limits of 3 to 40 seconds per problem, which capped a 12-problem trial at 8 minutes and left 159 levels with a par time above the cap, where a student working at the expected pace could never pass. A clock kept for a different number of problems is rescaled to keep its pace per problem.
