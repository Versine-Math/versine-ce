The speed a level's Level contribution and the parent page measure against is that level's par time, not a clock divided by a problem count. The old yardstick was 10 seconds per problem on nearly every level, so a student at the expected pace on about 600 of 665 levels scored no speed credit and read as below standard. The yardstick stays fixed for everyone and does not follow a student's own clock.
